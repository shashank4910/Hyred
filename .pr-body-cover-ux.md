## Summary
- On Workday cover-letter steps, Copilot now **hides the resume picker** and puts the cover letter front and center.
- Shows a **snippet** of the generated letter, **Preview** (opens full letter in a new tab), and **Edit on Hyred** (opens `/jobs/[matchId]`; guests go to login with `next=` redirect).
- Upload uses **PDF only** (`cover-letter.pdf`) — no `.txt` fallback on Workday file inputs.
- Detects when a cover letter is already uploaded on the page and updates status accordingly.
